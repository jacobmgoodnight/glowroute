Move your Holiday Display Explorer drives

Combine your old saved tours, local display collection, favorites, ratings and selected dated notes with your existing GlowRoute projects. Everything is processed in this browser; no files are uploaded.

1. Back up both apps first

  1. In the Explorer retirement checklist, follow the steps to export a complete private Explorer archive from each old browser or installed app. Ordinary displays.json is not enough for tours and pictures.
  2. In GlowRoute, open Manage saved drives & backups → Backup all years and save your existing GlowRoute JSON.
  3. Keep both originals and a second copy somewhere private. Do not share the Explorer archive or post it on GitHub.

2. Choose the two backup files

This creates a new merged backup, without modifying the originals or any saved data in your current GlowRoute tab. Nothing is written to local storage here.

3. Review and download

    Personal photos do not move into GlowRoute yet. They remain in the original private archive. Also check each address, season, starting point and route order after importing.

    To finish: open GlowRoute → Manage saved drives & backups → Restore backup, select this merged file, confirm the listed projects, and test a few drives before retiring Explorer. Restoring replaces that browser’s saved projects with the merged set.
